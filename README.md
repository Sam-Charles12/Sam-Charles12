## Who I am

I'm Carles. I study Electronics & Computer Engineering at LASU and I build software on the side, so I live somewhere between circuits and code. I like making things that actually work: simulators, tools, and apps people can use.

I'm leaning toward telecom and network engineering. I also run **The Circuit Room**, a peer-led ECE learning community I started in 2026.

## What I'm working on

- 🔌 **Logicraft**: a digital electronics simulator that runs in the browser
- 📡 **RF Forge**: an RF engineering tool ([live](https://rfforge.vercel.app/))
- 🧠 **Interact.**: an AI study tool that adapts to how you learn
- 📄 A research paper on picking wireless technologies for hospital networks

## Featured projects
